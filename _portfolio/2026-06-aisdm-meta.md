My initial [fellowship with AI-SDM](https://nikita-jaya.github.io/teaching/2025-cmu) transitioned into a collaboration on a research project through Carnegie Mellon University's [AI-SDM](https://www.cmu.edu/ai-sdm/) and Meta's AI for Good program. We Produced real-time dynamic situation reports aimed at helping first responders better address natural disasters like earthquakes, hurricanes and severe winter storms, utilizing Meta’s limited-access mobility datasets. We also explored the applications of computer vision techniques to provide actionable insights for on-the-ground usage during a disaster.


Here is the initial [press release](https://www.cmu.edu/news/stories/archives/2026/june/carnegie-mellon-and-meta-partner-to-develop-ai-tools-for-emergency-response).
